Privacy policy

What we collect, why we need it, and how to get it back or deleted.

Draft for review. Written to describe the data this site actually collects. Have it reviewed against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — and against your live analytics and payment providers — before publishing.

What we collect

  • Booking: name, phone, email, address, date of birth where age matters, certification level, and the medical information you declare.
  • Membership and volunteering: name, phone, email, city and state, preferred base.
  • Donations: name, email, phone if given, amount and frequency.
  • Shop: name, email, phone, delivery address.
  • Contact form: name, email, phone if given, and your message.

We do not collect card numbers, UPI IDs or bank details. Payments happen on our provider’s pages, and they never pass through this website.

Medical information

Medical declarations are sensitive personal data. We collect them only because they are required to run an activity safely. They are seen by the instructor or guide responsible for you and by the base manager, are not used for anything else, are never shared for marketing, and are deleted once the retention period below expires.

Why we hold it

  • To confirm and run your booking, membership or order
  • To meet safety, insurance and permit obligations
  • To send receipts and the season impact report
  • To answer your questions

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except the parties needed to deliver the service: our payment provider, our courier for shop orders, and the permit authorities for island trips.

Marketing

We email members and donors a season report and clean-up dates. Newsletter signup is opt-in. Every email carries an unsubscribe link that works immediately.

How long we keep it

  • Booking and medical records: three years after the activity, for insurance and liability purposes, then deleted.
  • Membership records: for the membership term plus two years.
  • Donation records: as long as accounting and tax rules require.
  • Contact messages: twelve months.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@dravaka.example and we will respond within 30 days. Deletion requests covering an activity within the liability window may be held until that window closes; we will tell you if that applies.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Any analytics used are configured without cross-site tracking, and the current configuration is described here before the site goes live.

Photography

We photograph trips for the gallery and social channels. Tell us and we will exclude you, or have an existing image removed — no reason needed.

Security

Data is held on access-controlled systems and shared internally only with the people who need it to do their job. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you we will tell you promptly and tell you what to do about it.

Contact

privacy@dravaka.example, or the contact page.
