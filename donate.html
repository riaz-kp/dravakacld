Foundation & donate

They can’t speak for themselves. You can.

The reef, the mangrove and the turtles have no way of asking. Everything they need has to come from someone who chose to give it.

How you can help

Donate your share. Change the destiny of a coastline.

Our trips pay for our trips. They do not pay for a nursery frame in April, a truckload of saplings before the rains, the disposal fee on two tonnes of mixed waste, or a school session in a town where nobody dives.

That is what donations are for. Every rupee goes to the mission side of Dravaka, and what it bought gets published at the end of the season.

Act & education

Why Dravaka, and what we do with the money

Dravaka is an adventure club with a foundation arm. The club sells dives, courses, treks and camps. The foundation runs the missions. They share people, boats and compressors, and they keep separate books.

That structure matters. It means the conservation work is not dependent on a good season, and it means a donation cannot quietly end up subsidising a boat charter. Mission spend is reported at the end of every season with the receipts behind it available to any member or donor who asks for them.

Two halves, always: act — the clean-ups, planting and nurseries; and education — the sessions that mean the town behind the beach keeps it clean when we are not there. Neither works alone.
