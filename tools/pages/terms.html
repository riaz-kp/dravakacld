Terms & conditions

The rules that apply when you book a trip, join the tribe, donate or buy from the shop.

Draft for review. This page is a working draft written to cover the activities described on this site. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have a qualified advocate check it against Indian consumer, tourism and adventure-operator law before the site goes live.

Last updated: this draft. Operated by Dravaka Adventure Club, Murudeshwar, Karnataka 581350, India.

1. Who we are

“Dravaka”, “we” and “us” mean Dravaka Adventure Club and its foundation arm. “You” means anyone booking an activity, holding a membership, making a donation or placing an order.

2. Booking policy

  • A booking request is not a confirmed booking. A place exists only once we confirm it in writing.
  • We take a 25% deposit after confirmation. The balance is due on the day of the activity unless agreed otherwise.
  • All participants must complete a signed liability and medical declaration before taking part. No form, no activity, no exceptions.
  • Government photo ID is required for every participant for boat manifests and, where applicable, island permits.
  • Prices are per person and may change for bookings not yet confirmed. A confirmed price is held.
  • Lakshadweep and Andaman trips depend on permits issued by third parties. We apply on your behalf; we cannot guarantee issue.

3. Safety and your responsibilities

Adventure activities carry inherent risk that cannot be removed. By taking part you accept that risk and agree to the following:

  • Declare all medical conditions, medication and pregnancy honestly at booking. Some conditions require written clearance from a doctor.
  • No alcohol for 12 hours before any dive, and no diving under the influence of alcohol or sedatives.
  • No flying for 18 hours after your last dive. Planning otherwise is your responsibility.
  • Follow the instructor or guide at all times. We may end an activity for any participant on safety grounds, without refund, if instructions are ignored.
  • Participants under 18 require a guardian’s signature; some activities have minimum ages listed on the activity pages.

We reserve the right to cancel, shorten or reroute any activity for weather, sea state, visibility, equipment or any other safety reason. Where we cancel, the refund policy applies.

4. Insurance

We carry public liability cover and operator insurance for our activities, staff and vessels. That cover is not a substitute for your own.

  • You are strongly advised to hold personal travel and accident insurance covering the activity you have booked, including diving to the depth involved.
  • Standard travel policies frequently exclude scuba diving, or cover it only to a stated depth. Check before you travel.
  • Our cover does not extend to your medical costs, evacuation, trip cancellation, or loss of or damage to your personal equipment.
  • Diver evacuation and chamber treatment can be expensive. Specialist diving cover is inexpensive and we recommend it for every diver.

5. Equipment

Hire equipment is serviced at the start of each season and pressure-tested where applicable. You are responsible for equipment issued to you and for reporting any fault before entering the water. Loss or damage through negligence may be charged at replacement cost.

6. Membership

Memberships run for twelve months from confirmation. Benefits are as described on the membership page and may be varied with notice. We do not auto-renew unless you ask us to. Membership may be withdrawn without refund for conduct that endangers others or the environment.

7. Donations

Donations fund the mission programmes described on the missions page. Administration is capped at 7.5% of donation income. Donations are not refundable except where made in error — see the refund policy. Monthly donations can be cancelled at any time.

8. Shop orders

Orders are subject to stock. Where an item is unavailable after payment we refund it in full. Returns are covered by the return policy. Risk passes to you on delivery.

9. Payments

Payments are processed by a third-party payment provider on their own secure pages. We do not collect, store or process card or UPI credentials on this website. Their terms apply to the payment transaction itself.

10. Conduct and the environment

On every Dravaka activity: nothing living is taken, nothing is touched or stood on underwater, and all waste comes back out with you. We do not supply single-use plastic and ask that you do not bring it. Participants who ignore this may be asked to leave without refund.

11. Photography

We photograph and film trips for the gallery and social channels. Tell us at booking or on the day if you do not wish to appear, and we will exclude you.

12. Liability

Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for fraud, where such limitation is not permitted by law. Subject to that, our liability in connection with any booking is limited to the amount you paid for it.
