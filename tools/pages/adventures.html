Scuba diving

The main event

Scuba is what Dravaka was built around. Everything below is run by instructors who dive these sites weekly, not seasonally.

Groups are capped at four students per instructor for training and six divers per guide for fun dives. Every programme includes a briefing on the site’s condition and what we are doing about it — you will usually surface having collected something.

Worth knowing

Four things about scuba most people get wrong

You do not need to be a strong swimmer

For a try dive you need to be comfortable in water, not fast in it. Certification requires a 200 m swim with no time limit and a 10-minute float.

Colour disappears before light does

Red is gone by about 5 m, orange by 10 m. That is why blood looks green underwater and why a torch changes a reef completely.

The most dangerous depth is the last 5 m

Pressure changes fastest near the surface. Almost every ear problem and every fast-ascent incident happens in water shallow enough to stand up in later.

Good buoyancy is a conservation skill

A diver who can hover does not kick the reef. We spend more course time on buoyancy than the standards require, for exactly this reason.
