About us

An adventure club that took on a second job.

Dravaka runs dives, treks and camps on the Indian coast — and puts the proceeds and the people into keeping that coast worth visiting.

About Dravaka

Where it started

A group of us used to dive the same handful of sites off the Karnataka coast every season. Over about four years we watched the same thing happen at each one: more plastic in the water column, more bleached heads on the reef, less of the fish life that made the site worth the boat ride.

You can respond to that in two ways. You can find somewhere else to dive, or you can do something about the place you already love. Dravaka is the second option, organised properly.

We are an adventure club first. We teach scuba from the first breath through to instructor level, we run snorkelling and free diving, and we take people trekking and camping inland when the sea is closed. The conservation work is not a side project bolted on for appearances — it is scheduled, budgeted and measured like everything else we run.

What Dravaka is doing

Six lines of work, running all year

None of it is dramatic. It is the same work, done on a schedule, in the same places, long enough to show up in the water.

Waste management

Everything we collect is sorted and weighed on site, then routed to local recyclers or safe disposal. We publish the tonnage.

Pollution control

Water sampling at our six bases, plus pressure on the specific upstream sources that keep showing up in the results.

Coral regeneration

Rope and frame nurseries off Netrani and Kaup, with fragments of opportunity grown out and returned to the reef.

Beach clean-ups

First Saturday of every month, six bases, open to anyone. Members, guests, schools, whoever turns up.

Mangrove cultivation

Nursery-raised saplings planted along estuary banks with the local panchayats, then monitored for three seasons.

Educational awareness

Free sessions for schools, fishing cooperatives and resorts on waste, tides, reef health and what it is worth locally.

How it affects our environment

What actually changes when this work gets done

Five systems, and the specific difference each one sees. This is the positive side — and it is measurable.

Ecosystem

Remove the plastic and the ghost nets and the food web reassembles from the bottom up. Grazers come back to cleared substrate, predators follow the grazers, and the site starts holding fish between seasons instead of emptying out.

Ocean

Less floating debris means less of it fragmenting into pieces small enough to be eaten. Clearer water lets light reach the coral, and coral that gets light has a chance of recovering from a bleaching event rather than dying after it.

Nature and the coastline

A planted mangrove belt holds the bank through monsoon, filters run-off before it reaches the reef, and gives juvenile fish somewhere to grow up. The village behind it loses less land each year.

Air

Mangroves and coastal vegetation lock away carbon at several times the rate of inland forest per hectare. Waste that gets recycled instead of burned on the beach is smoke that nobody has to breathe.

Plants and dune systems

Clearing debris lets dune grasses re-root, and rooted dunes stop the sand walking inland. It is unglamorous work with a visible result inside two seasons.

People

Divers, fishers and resort staff who can point at a specific reef and say what it was like three years ago are the ones who protect it. Most of our volunteers started as guests on a try dive.

What you can do in Dravaka

Five ways in

Pick whichever one fits the time you have.

Book an adventure

A try dive, a course, a fun dive, a trek or a camp. Every booking carries a share into the mission fund.

Join the tribe

Membership gets you priority on trips, discounted courses and a standing invitation to every clean-up we run.

Volunteer

Turn up to a clean-up, a planting weekend or a nursery maintenance dive. No membership needed, no experience needed.

Donate

Fund the parts that do not pay for themselves: nursery frames, sapling stock, school sessions, disposal fees.

Buy from the shop

Gear and merchandise we actually use. The margin goes to the same fund as everything else.
