Medical cancellations

If you cannot dive for a medical reason confirmed in writing by a doctor, we refund in full whenever you tell us, including on the day. We would much rather you did not dive.

Part-completed courses

If you start a course and cannot finish it, you can complete it with us within twelve months at no extra charge. Where that is not possible we refund the unused portion less certification fees already paid to the training agency on your behalf.

Permits and third-party costs

Lakshadweep and Andaman permits, ferry tickets and third-party accommodation are refunded only to the extent that the issuing party refunds us. We will show you what we recovered.

How refunds are paid

To the original payment method, within 7–10 working days of us confirming the refund. Bank timings are outside our control. We do not deduct a processing fee.

Memberships

Cancel within 14 days of joining for a full refund, provided you have not used a member benefit. After that, memberships are non-refundable but can be transferred to another person once per term — just tell us who.

Donations

Donations are not generally refundable, as the money is committed to mission work quickly. If you donated in error, or an amount you did not intend, write to foundation@dravaka.example within 30 days and we will refund it without argument. Monthly donations can be cancelled at any time and stop from the next cycle.

Shop returns

  • 14 days from delivery to return anything unused and in its original packaging.
  • Hygiene exception: masks, snorkels, rash guards and anything worn against the skin can only be returned unopened.
  • Faulty items: returned or replaced at our cost, whenever the fault appears, within the manufacturer’s warranty.
  • Return shipping is yours unless the item was faulty or we sent the wrong thing.
  • Refunds are issued within 7 working days of the item reaching us.

To start a return, email shop@dravaka.example with your order number.

Contact

Anything not covered here: talk to us. If a situation is genuinely unfair and the policy above does not cover it, tell us — we would rather fix it than hide behind a table.
