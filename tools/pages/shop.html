Common questions

How long does delivery take?

Three to six working days across India. Island deliveries to Lakshadweep and the Andamans take longer and we will tell you how long before you pay.

Can I return something?

Yes, unused and in its packaging, within 14 days. Masks and anything worn against the skin can only be returned unopened, for hygiene reasons. Full detail on the return policy.

Do you ship outside India?

Not yet. Email the team if you want something sent abroad and we will quote it individually.

Is the “funds X kg of clean-up” figure real?

It is a calculated estimate, not a marketing line. We divide the mission budget by the tonnage recovered to get a cost per kilogram, then apply each product’s margin against it. The working is in the season report.
