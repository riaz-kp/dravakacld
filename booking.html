Do you have to pay now?

No. We confirm your place first. Once confirmed we send a secure payment link for a 25% deposit, with the balance due on the day. Courses over ₹50,000 can be split across two instalments.

We accept UPI, cards and bank transfer through a payment gateway. Card details are never entered on this site and never handled by us.

Refunds, briefly

  • Cancel 14+ days out — full refund of the deposit
  • 7–13 days — 50% refund or a free reschedule
  • Under 7 days — no refund, one reschedule within the season
  • We cancel for weather or safety — full refund or reschedule, your choice

Before you arrive

Bring ID

Government photo ID for everyone in the party. Mandatory for boat manifests and permits.

Sleep and hydrate

No alcohol for 12 hours before a dive. Dehydration is the most common cause of a cut-short first dive.

No flying for 18 hours

After your last dive. Plan the trip home around it, not the other way round.

Leave the plastic

We do not supply single-use bottles. Bring something refillable.
