Welcome to the tribe

Join the tribe.

Membership pays for the work that happens between trips. It also gets you first call on every trip we run.

A club only works if enough people are in it when there is nothing exciting on. Members are the reason the nurseries get maintained in April and the saplings get watered in May, and why we can promise a school a session in a month when nobody has booked a dive.

In return, members get the things that are genuinely scarce: places on the good trips, small-group ratios, and afternoon slots on the mission dives that we cannot sell to walk-ins.

What happens to your money

  • 60% — mission costs: bags, gloves, disposal fees, nursery materials, sapling stock
  • 25% — the education programme and its travel
  • 15% — running the club: insurance, permits, admin

Published in the season report, with the receipts behind it available to any member who asks.

Let’s do it together

Become a Dravaka volunteer

No fee, no membership, no experience. If you can carry a bag up a beach you are qualified.

Clean-ups run on the first Saturday of every month at all six bases, 6:30 am, done by 10. Planting weekends run through the pre-monsoon months. Nursery maintenance dives need a current certification and reasonable buoyancy — we will check both, kindly.

  • Gloves, bags, a scale and breakfast provided
  • Bring water in something refillable and closed shoes
  • Under-16s welcome with an adult
  • Certificates and letters for students who need the hours
